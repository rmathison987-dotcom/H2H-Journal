Decisions baked into this

Things worth confirming before any of it goes to a printer.

DecisionWhat I did
Questions printedPrinted on the page, per your ask. Note this reverses the earlier call to show only the temperature — it means the journal must be reprinted if card wording ever changes.
Green deckExcluded. 30 icebreakers, which we agreed don't need journaling — that's why this is 60 entries and not 90.
Blue orderingUses your Updated list (hardest → lightest), mapped −10° to 48° in 2° steps. The outdated list's temperatures no longer match after the reorder.
Red orderingAs written in the doc, 82° to 140° in 2° steps. No updated Red list exists yet.
DirectionColdest at the front cover, hottest at the back, neutral where they meet in the middle. The extremes sit at the outer edges.
CoverRebuilt to the box artwork — gradient, contour system, wordmark and mascot all matched to the packaging. Contours are generated, so they can be reflowed to any trim size without redrawing.
Back cover copySet verbatim as supplied, in the box's layout: heading, description beneath it, heart and MADE WITH LOVE at the foot.
Centre pagesNow carry the card colours — Introspective blue on the left, Interpersonal amber on the right, with the same contour texture as the deck.
Copy left exactly as you sent it Two things in the back-cover text I did not change, in case they're deliberate: “that Feels honest to you” has a capital F mid-sentence, and there's no comma after “what truly matters”. Say the word and I'll correct both.
Three copy fixes I made silently in the questions Blue 28° read "before you falling asleep" — corrected to "before you fall asleep". Blue 0° was missing its question mark. Blue 40° carried a stray quotation mark. All three are in your Updated list and should be fixed at source too.
One gap worth closing Your Blue deck has an Updated pass with a full change log and quality scores; Red doesn't. Red is still running on the original list, so it hasn't had the same editing scrutiny — worth a pass before print, since these are the 30 heaviest questions in the game.
